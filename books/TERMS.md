**Last updated: 2026-11-02**

One line before you scroll: **Nazra is an informational tool — not a legal authority
and not a substitute for the law.**

### 1. Nature of the service
- Nazra displays information about speed-camera locations and average-speed
  monitoring corridors — 10 corridors and 177 camera records (fixed radars,
  traffic-light cameras, checkpoints, unmarked bumps) — sourced from
  OpenStreetMap and Amman municipality (GAM) April-2026 announcements, with
  community reports stored on-device.
- Displayed values (speed limits, alert distances, camera counts) are **informational
  only**; they can be stale or wrong. Fines and violations are decided by applicable
  Jordanian traffic law, never by this screen.

### 2. User conduct and responsibility
- **You are the responsible driver.** Obey posted limits, stop when required, watch

- Using Nazra does not excuse inattention, and driving decisions always belong to
  the person holding the wheel.

### 3. Community reports are unverified by default
- Reports come from users and open map data; every entry is flagged "not

  completeness, or coverage.
- Fabricated or noisy reports are prohibited. There is no backend to enforce this
